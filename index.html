Platform Overview

Built for the Blue Frontier

VBM (Vanguard Bluewater Marine) is a dynamically positioned semi-submersible drilling unit commissioned for ultra-deepwater campaigns. Named for its pioneering operator consortium, the rig combines sixth-generation drilling systems with integrated subsea production tie-back capability.

Stationed in the North Atlantic, VBM supports exploration and appraisal wells in water depths where conventional jack-ups cannot operate. Our crew of 180 specialists maintains 24/7 operations with full SIMOPS certification.

From well control to environmental monitoring, every system on VBM is designed around a single principle: precision under pressure.
